# Alpha v0.9 — Fresh Start, Daily Life, and Player Freedom

Alpha v0.9 replaces the garage-first new-run premise with an Anchorage arrival at Yalonda and John's home. New players begin with $1,000 cash, zero Heat, Dre's fixed $1,200 Day 7 note, and no product, weapon, vehicle, property, local standing, or Rook attention. Save version 3 and `907ogr_v3` are unchanged; hydrated v3 saves keep their established assets and premise.

## Playable systems

- Four primary tabs: Market, Places, People, and More.
- Places is available on Day 1 and contains home management, Ship Creek work, Spenard exploration, the Night Owl, gym training, Northern Value shoplifting, the discoverable informal game, People Mover transit, Industrial access status, and browse-only property listings.
- Ship Creek pays $110 plus a seeded $15–30 opportunity, once per Morning. Standing 3 grants legal cover.
- Gym sessions cost $25/$45/$75/$120 and add 3/2/1/1 hidden progress to Strength, Endurance, or Reflexes. Attribute gains require 10, then 18 additional, then 28 additional progress and cap at 5.
- Gambling uses $20/$50/$100 cash stakes, seeded attribute-informed approaches, negative expected value, and no debt.
- Northern Value supports one small Reflexes-led shoplifting attempt daily, modified by Insight, Heat, and remembered suspicion.
- The first Explore Spenard action guarantees supplier discovery. The next reveals the informal game; later discoveries diminish.
- Bus rides cost $5 and consume one part of day. Passes cost $12/day or $45/week. Downtown opens by bus; Industrial remains route-gated.
- North Star Garage costs a $650 deposit with the first week included. It is optional; storage, upgrades, protected recovery, crew operations, and dependent story scenes unlock only after acquisition.

## Household and progression

Yalonda and John are recurring People. The home safely stores cash, up to two product units total, and one concealable weapon. Overnight seeded discovery begins at 10% for one contraband item, adds 8 percentage points per additional item, adds 2 points per Heat above 2, and adds 10 points at Heat 6+. Minor violations add one warning, serious violations add two, and catastrophic danger can evict immediately. Three warnings end the run as **Nowhere to Go**. John provides one free local question daily.

Street Read is run-scoped and separate from Street Identity and Operation Score. Its thresholds are 40, 110, 210, and 340 XP. Deduplicated awards cover first work, training, supplier discovery, profitable trade, shoplifting success, gambling participation, bus use, property acquisition, and established accomplishments. Level-ups use the existing feed; Street Read appears only under More.

## Story continuity

Mara is an optional stranger met for the first time at the Night Owl, with the exact opening tones Friendly honesty, Light flirtation, and Brief and guarded. Her sedan threat is a later consequence requiring established continuity and player-created Rook pressure. Kip begins unknown and is discovered through play. Rook begins unaware at zero pressure. Garage-dependent beats require garage control.
