## 2. Refinements to the analysis (things the data adds)

### 2a. `stickup` is a hidden counterexample the table's "…" swallows

`stickup`: Deshawn 0%, rentMissed 0.3, evictions 0% — it breaks the plan's
"no Deshawn → misses accrue → eviction" story. The reason: its runs end at
**avg day 7** (7/8 arrested) — before rent can miss even once. The eviction table
is **right-censored by other death causes**, and the build prompt should say so,
or the next session will read `stickup` at 0% evictions as "solvent."

### 2b. The censoring cuts the other way too, and it bounds guard condition 3

End causes at 40 days, 8 seeds each: `legal_worker`, `flipper`, `broker`, and
`worker` all die `killed` in **8 of 8 runs**, around day 35–38. The four purely
legal strategies — heatCap 2, no combat — all die. Whatever the health drain is,
it is systematic, it kills solvent law-abiding runs on a near-fixed schedule, and
it caps how long any eviction ladder can be observed. Guard condition 3's
"5–20% eviction band" is therefore measured against a censoring process; that is
acceptable for this build, but the acceptance criteria should note that the band
is conditional on the current (unexplained) death schedule, which v1.33 will change.

This also **strengthens Task 4's priority**: health attrition is very likely
itself a run-length-bounded assumption (a drain that could never kill anyone in
10 days), i.e., Task 4 is a specific instance of Task 3's hazard class.

### 2c. Option B needs no schema change — confirmed by derivation

The plan asked whether B ("grace defers one day") can be derived rather than
stored. It can, with the two existing v11 fields:

- On the due-day night with grace available: consume the grace
  (`extraRentGraceAvailable = false`, `extraRentGraceUsedDueDay = currentRentDue`)
  but **do not set `lastMissedDueDay`**.
- The nightly check re-fires the next crossing; grace is gone, so the miss lands —
  exactly one day late. `PAY_RENT` during the deferral day advances `rentDueDay`
  past `run.day`, so the check goes quiet — the grace genuinely bought one more
  day to pay, which is what the authored copy already says.
- Re-arm (if kept per-period) still keys off `extraRentGraceUsedDueDay !== currentRentDue`.

Note that under B, a re-arming grace is no longer an infinite subsidy — it defers,
never cancels — so **B makes the A-vs-"re-arm forever" fight mostly moot**: Deshawn
can keep talking to Yalonda every week and rent still lands every week. That is the
strongest argument for B and the plan undersells it.

### 2d. Task 2's mechanism note

Un-gating `workerTurn` gives every strategy the `PAY_RENT`/`PAY_PHONE_BILL`
dispatches but **not** the `marketFloat` bill reserve — that only shapes behavior
for market strategies. Cash-poor strategies (`trainer`, `trader`, `cautious`) will
still miss rent because their wealth is in inventory or the gym, which is the honest
measurement the plan wants. The build prompt should state explicitly that Task 2 is
"pay when cash is on hand on the due day," not "reserve for bills" — so the next
session doesn't add reserves to every profile and over-correct.

Also: `workerTurn` returns after the bills section for non-employment profiles
(`if(!p.employment)return null` at line 190), so un-gating it is behaviorally safe —
non-worker strategies get bills only, no accidental job-seeking.

### 2e. Task 4's "nobody knows why" has a prime suspect, and it is the rent trap again

The plan is right to measure rather than fix — but the measurement must account
for this or v1.33 will tune engine health constants against a broken instrument:

- **The harness has exactly one healing dispatch in its entire history** —
  `NILE_WELLNESS` at `simulate-runs.js:348`, inside the gambler-mode Nile branch.
  14 of 15 strategies never heal, ever — the same shape as "14 of 15 never
  dispatched `PAY_RENT`."
- The engine has **no passive health regeneration**; recovery is entirely
  elective and mostly paid (`SLEEP_HOME` +12, first aid, clinic, Nile wellness),
  and steady drains exist that legal strategies hit daily (`work_hard` shifts
  cost −2 health each at `game-core.js:7274`, `WALK_HOME` −3 at `8691`,
  per-slot encounter rolls with 10–25 damage on a loss).
- The end-cause data matches exactly: `gambler` — the one strategy that heals —
  has **zero** `killed` deaths and reaches day 40; `legal_worker`/`flipper`/
  `broker`/`worker` — solvent, low-heat, never healing — die `killed` 8/8
  around day 35–38 (≈ −2 to −3/day from full health).

So Task 4's telemetry should record **healing dispatched** alongside health lost
per source, and the build prompt should state up front that "killed dominance"
is probably strategy coverage, not engine balance — before v1.33 inherits it as
an economy-of-health problem. Whether Task 2's scope should also give
strategies a minimal heal rule (the workerTurn treatment for health) is a real
question for Davon: doing it makes 40-day economics measurable at all for legal
strategies; not doing it keeps this build honest about only touching rent.
Recommendation: leave healing out of v1.32 (one variable at a time — that
discipline is why this plan's numbers are trustworthy), but say in the prompt
that the third row of the payment table is still right-censored by unhealed
attrition at ~day 35.

### 2f. One Task 3 candidate is more benign than cited

`weeklyIncomeEstimate` (`game-core.js:4222`) divides by elapsed days and multiplies
by 7 — that is a weekly-**rate** normalization, valid at any run length. Only its
comment ("The run is only 7 days") is stale. The story scheduler
(`STORY_BEATS_PER_DAY` at 5683/5985) is the real one: the cap was authored to stop
every run resolving every storyline by day 4, and at 40 days every long run resolves
every storyline anyway, just by ~day 20. The cap now paces, it no longer rations.

## 3. Answers to the plan's four reviewer questions

1. **Grace shape: B**, and more firmly than the plan's own lean. It matches the
   authored copy, needs no schema bump (§2c), keeps Deshawn's tier-1 value real
   (one extra day to find $150 is worth something every single week), and keeps
   eviction reachable. A is acceptable as a fallback if B's implementation fights
   the nightly-block ordering, but the derivation above suggests it won't.

2. **Universal `payBills`, plus one explicit non-paying control — but pick the
   control for lifespan.** `stickup` already functions as an accidental non-payer
   and is useless as a control (arrested by day 7, never reaches the ladder — §2a).
   The control must be a profile that survives past day 29 without paying;
   `trainer` already fills that role economically (can't pay ≠ won't pay, but both
   exercise the ladder). Recommendation: make `payBills` default-on in the shared
   turn loop, add `skipBills: true` to **one** long-surviving profile, and document
   on the profile that it is the eviction-ladder instrument.

3. **`trainer` at 50% is acceptable and should ship as a finding, not a fix.**
   It is the strategy that genuinely cannot afford rent, which is exactly the
   signal an obligation system should produce. Gym pricing is v1.33 balance work;
   the plan's own out-of-scope rule covers it. State it in PROJECT_STATUS as a
   measured outcome so it isn't refiled as a bug.

4. **Do the Task 3 sweep in this build, as inventory-only** — the sweep below
   (§4) found enough to confirm the hazard class is real but bounded; a separate
   audit build would mostly re-do this reading. Cap it: inventory in the docs,
   fixes only where the mechanic interacts with rent/eviction (this build's
   subject), everything else filed for v1.33+.

## 4. Task 3 head start — sweep results

A very-thorough sweep of `game-core.js`, `encounters.js`, and `src/` was run for
this review. **These are candidates from a reading pass, not confirmed findings**
— the ones marked ✓ were independently verified during this review; the rest
follow the project rule: re-run the check, don't inherit the claim. The plan's
premise ("the Deshawn grace will not be the only one") is confirmed: the hazard
class is real, but bounded — the inventory below is likely close to complete
(see coverage notes at the end).

### Tier 1 — likely breaks balance at 40 days

1. **Dre's note compounds daily with no ceiling, plus +1 Heat per late day**
   (`game-core.js:5553–5571`). `fee = max(25, balance*0.08) × multipliers`,
   added to balance every crossed day past due. ~3 firings inside a 10-day run;
   up to ~33 now. The daily +1 Heat alone reaches the Heat-15 terminal in ~2
   weeks because Heat has no passive decay (see #4).
2. **`missed_obligation` observations are deliberately un-clamped and linear**
   (`src/data/observations.js:113–118`); Dre gets one per penalty day, so his
   disposition is driven unboundedly negative over a long unpaid stretch, past
   every band floor, with no recovery path.
3. **Household warnings never decay** ✓ (`game-core.js:6359–6370`) — misses at
   days 7/14/21/28 give deterministic eviction at ~day 28–29 (matches
   PROJECT_STATUS's v1.26 number), and `checkHomeContraband` rolls every night
   against home-stored goods, each hit a warning. This is the intended lose
   condition and the mirror of the grace bug: with Deshawn it never fires,
   without him it reliably terminates. v1.32's Task 1/2 own this pair.
4. **Heat is a one-way ratchet** — the nightly block decays `financialHeat`,
   Curtis awareness, and plug suspicion, but never `player.heat`; decay is
   entirely elective (`LAY_LOW`, arrest relief, Deshawn de-escalation) while
   nightly +1 sources are numerous. Invisible at 7 days; terminal at 40.
5. **Health is strictly monotonic downward** ✓ — no passive regen anywhere;
   all recovery is elective/paid; steady drains include −2 per `work_hard`
   shift (`7274`) and −3 `WALK_HOME` (`8691`). See §2e — the likely `killed`
   driver, jointly with harness coverage.
6. **Per-slot encounter rolls scale linearly with run length**
   (`game-core.js:6468–6474`, `encounters.js:210–227`) — ~28 rolls per run
   became ~160, each loss 10–25 damage. The volume multiplier on #5.

### Tier 2 — drift worth filing, not fixing here

7. Territory `dailyIncome` (up to ~$230/day, no ongoing cost) against flat
   weekly obligations — income scales with days, costs with weeks; by day 40
   cash stops binding any risk decision (`5466–5481`, `4694–4772`).
8. `stickHeatMultiplier = 1 + 0.1 × heatStreak`, streak unbounded (`2395`).
9. `weeklyIncomeEstimate` at `4222` ✓ — the math is a valid weekly rate at any
   length; only the "run is only 7 days" comment is stale. The other four
   `RUN_DAYS` uses are inert defaults/clamps ✓.
10. `ATTRIBUTE_MAX = 12` is now reachable, flattening late-run checks tuned
    against a 7-day ceiling of ~+2.
11. Deshawn's weekly intro degenerates into an infinite weekly `reliable: true`
    rumor faucet once the ~4 real intros are exhausted (~day 28)
    (`5247–5288`) — a second unbounded Deshawn perk, worth handling alongside
    Task 1 or filing next to it.
12. The only two story beats with `latest: {day: 6}` ceilings are Mina stages
    2–3 (`5721, 5725`); missing them locks the entire Mina chain and both her
    endings for the remaining 34 days.
13. Story pacing ✓ (`STORY_BEATS_PER_DAY`, `5683/5985`): the cap authored to
    ration a 7-day registry now only paces it — every long run exhausts every
    `once: true` beat by ~day 20, then repeats ambient beats ~15–20× each.
14. `lateRunDay()` fires the authored *finale* beats (`curtis_day7`,
    `mina_after`) at ~day 10 — the 25% mark of a 40-day run (`2537–2545`).

### Tier 3 — hygiene

Unbounded per-day history arrays (`stats.visits`, `lender.penaltyHistory`,
`shark.history` — contrast the capped `state.log`); the exposure ledger's
`MAX_LEDGER_ROWS = 120` silently drops observations once 40 days of nightly
rows saturate it, freezing an NPC's disposition; assorted once-per-day grants
(street-read intel, disclosure calls, Pherris's daily tier-3 income) now fire
~40× instead of ~7×. Also an *absence*: the exposure engine has no recency
window or relationship decay at all — append-only forever — which is itself a
run-length assumption, just not a mistuned constant.

### Sweep coverage (what turned up nothing)

`re-arm` appears exactly once in the codebase — the known Deshawn case; no
second re-arming grant exists. No `once per run` comments exist. All ~100
`last*Day` fields were enumerated: every one is a same-day guard or fixed
window except the grace pair. `checkpointDay`/`lateRunDay` readers match what
`tests/v1-31.test.js` asserts. `encounters.js` has no difficulty/reward term
that reads `run.day`.

**Scope recommendation confirmed:** inventory in docs, fix only #3's rent side

they are one cluster ("pressure and attrition were tuned against a 10-day

## 5. Additions the plan should make (none blocking)

- **Name the censoring** (§2a/2b) in the build prompt and in the acceptance
  criteria for guard 3.
- **Task 4 telemetry should record the end day** alongside per-source health loss,
  because eviction and death race each other (~day 29 vs ~day 35) and neither
  number is interpretable without the other.
- **Guard condition for Deshawn himself.** Whatever grace shape ships, add one
  assertion that Deshawn's recruitment still changes a measurable outcome
  (e.g., a run with him misses ≥1 fewer rent or survives ≥1 day longer on a
  fixed seed). Under A especially, it would be easy to ship a Deshawn whose
  tier-1 perk is dead weight and not notice — the same "passing tests, dead
  mechanic" shape this project keeps finding.
- **Rewrite both grace tests**, not one: `tests/v1-15.test.js:568` (re-arm) and
  the adjacent "no re-arm after Deshawn departs" — under B the departed case's
  semantics change too (a banked grace still only defers).
- The copy fix (§ "one more day") comes free with B; under A or C the log line
  must be reauthored, and that is a STORY_BIBLE-voice edit, not a mechanical one.
